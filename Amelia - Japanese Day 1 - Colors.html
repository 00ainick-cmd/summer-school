Colors
いろ

Learn to recognize color words in Japanese. Some are kanji (赤 = red) and some are borrowed words written in katakana (ピンク = pink).

What this lesson teachesI can identify the general topic and some basic information by recognizing practiced or memorized words, phrases, and simple sentences.

04

Good to know

Good to know
Not every color is a kanji.
Older color words use kanji: 赤 (aka, red), 青 (ao, blue). Newer ones borrowed from English are written in katakana: ピンク (pinku, pink), オレンジ (orenji, orange).
