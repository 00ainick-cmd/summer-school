Rounding to the Nearest 10 and 100

Rounding finds the closest ten or hundred. The number line shows you which one is nearer. The midpoint is the tie-breaker: halfway or more rounds up.

What this lesson teachesRound whole numbers to the nearest 10 or 100.
01

Round to the nearest 10

Look where the number sits. Is it closer to the lower ten or the higher ten? The dashed line is the halfway mark.

02

Round to the nearest 100

Same idea, bigger jumps. Closer to the lower hundred or the higher hundred?

03

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
To round 47 to the nearest 10, look at the 4.
No. To round to the nearest ten, look at the digit to the right of the tens, the ones digit. In 47 that is 7. Seven is 5 or more, so round up to 50.
