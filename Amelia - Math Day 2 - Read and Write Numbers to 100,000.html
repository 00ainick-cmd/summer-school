Beat 1 · Hook

Read big numbers like a pro

Tap each card to see how a big number breaks into pieces. The comma is a tiny signal that tells you where the thousands start.

Beat 2 · What you will be able to do

Your goal today

  • 1Name every place from ones up to ten thousands.
  • 2Read any number up to 100,000 out loud.
  • 3Write the same number in standard form, word form, and expanded form.
Why it matters: big numbers live everywhere, like ticket counts at a game or the views on a video. Once you know the places, you can read any of them.
The objective (verbatim standard)Read, write and identify whole numbers within 100,000 using base ten numerals, number names and expanded form.
