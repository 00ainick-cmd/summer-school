Beat 4 · Teach (why it works)
LEARN 2

Why "add a zero" works

Multiplying by 10 makes the value 10 times bigger. Every digit shifts one place to the left. So 12 becomes ...

Each digit moves one place left, so the ones digit becomes a tens digit, the tens digit becomes hundreds, and a 0 fills the new ones spot. That looks like adding a zero.
Beat 4 · Teach (one step or two)
LEARN 3

Count the zeros

For 40 x 30, how many zeros end up in the answer?

Each "by 10" step adds one zero. 40 x 30 = (4 x 3) x 10 x 10 = 12 x 100 = 1,200.
Beat 4 · Teach (watch out)
LEARN 4

Two zeros, not one

For 6 x 70, the answer is 420 (one zero attached). For 60 x 70 the answer is ...

6 x 7 = 42. Then count zeros: 60 has one zero, 70 has one zero. Two zeros total. 4,200.
