Beat 1 · Hook

What does 5 x 7 really mean?

A times sign is a story. 5 x 7 means "5 groups of 7." Tap each card to see two stories that both come out to 35, and notice what makes them different.

5 bags, 7 apples each
5 groups of 7. The whole is 35 apples. Five bags on the counter.
7 bags, 5 apples each
7 groups of 5. Still 35 apples, but a different scene: seven smaller bags.
Both end at 35, but the situation is different. "5 x 7" tells the story: 5 groups of 7. Order matters when you read a problem.

Beat 4 · Teach (groups of)
LEARN 1

Read 3 x 4 as "three groups of four"

The first number tells you how many groups. The second tells you how many in each group.

3 groups of 4 = 12
Big idea: 3 x 4 is the same as 4 + 4 + 4. Multiplication is just a faster way to add equal groups.
Beat 4 · Teach (arrays)
LEARN 2

An array is also "groups of"

Look at this array: 3 rows of 5 squares. That is 3 groups of 5, or 3 x 5 = 15. Each row is one group.

Rows are the groups; columns are how many in each group. 3 rows of 5 = 3 x 5 = 15.
Beat 4 · Teach (order matters in the story)
LEARN 3

Same answer, different scene

5 x 7 and 7 x 5 both equal 35. But in a word problem the order tells the story.

5 x 7

5 bags, 7 apples in each. 5 bags on the counter.

7 x 5

7 bags, 5 apples in each. 7 smaller bags.

"Plates" is the number of groups (4). "Cookies on each plate" is how many in each group (6). So 4 groups of 6, or 4 x 6.
Beat 4 · Teach (read the word problem)
LEARN 4

Find the groups and the size

"There are 6 nail polish bottles in each case. Amelia has 3 cases. How many bottles?"

"Cases" is the number of groups (3). "Bottles in each case" is how many in each group (6). 3 groups of 6 = 18.

Beat 12 · The big takeaway
SYNTHESIS

The one rule to keep

Read a x b as "a groups of b."

The first number is the count of groups. The second is the size of each group. The total is the product.
