Day 5 · Week 2

Properties of operations

A "property" is a rule that is always true about numbers. Multiplication has three properties that turn hard problems into easy ones. Today you meet all three.

By the end you will use the commutative, associative, and distributive properties to multiply faster.

What this lesson teaches Apply properties of operations as strategies to multiply and divide.
01

Commutative property: order can flip

When you multiply two numbers, the order does not change the product. 4 x 7 is the same as 7 x 4. Both equal 28.

Commutative property of multiplication
4 x 7 = 7 x 4
Picture an array of 4 rows of 7 dots, then turn your head sideways. Same dots, now 7 rows of 4. Same total.
Why this helps: if you know 7 x 4 but not 4 x 7, you already know both. Flip and use the one you remember.
02

Associative property: grouping can shift

When you multiply three numbers, you can group them in any way. The answer stays the same.

Associative property of multiplication
(2 x 3) x 4 = 2 x (3 x 4)
Multiply the inside parentheses first. (2 x 3) x 4 is 6 x 4 = 24. And 2 x (3 x 4) is 2 x 12 = 24. Same answer.
Why this helps: sometimes one grouping is easier. For 5 x 7 x 2, group the 5 x 2 first to get 10 x 7 = 70. Way faster.
03

Distributive property: split, then add

A tricky multiplication can be broken into two easier pieces. Multiply each piece, then add.

Distributive property
5 x (3 + 2) = (5 x 3) + (5 x 2)
5 x 5 is 25. Or split it: 5 x 3 is 15, 5 x 2 is 10, total is 25. Same answer.
Real use: 6 x 8 is the same as 6 x (5 + 3). That is (6 x 5) + (6 x 3) = 30 + 18 = 48. Two friendly facts beat one hard one.
04

Try it: name the property

Tap the property the equation shows.

1. 8 x 5 = 5 x 8

When the order of two factors flips, that is the commutative property.

2. 4 x (5 + 3) = (4 x 5) + (4 x 3)

A factor multiplied by a sum can be broken apart. That is the distributive property.

3. (2 x 4) x 5 = 2 x (4 x 5)

When three factors stay in the same order but the parentheses shift, that is the associative property.
