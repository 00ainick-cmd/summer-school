Number lab · Grade 3 Standard 3.RA.C.7
Day 1 · Week 3

Multiply and divide fluently

"Fluent" means fast and correct. You know your facts so well you do not have to count on your fingers. Today you sharpen the facts you know and learn smart strategies for the ones that feel slow.

By the end you will use the multiplication chart and the link between times and divide to answer any fact within 100.

What this lesson teaches Multiply and divide with numbers and results within 100 using strategies such as the relationship between multiplication and division or properties of operations. Know all products of two one-digit numbers.
01

What "fluent" means

Fluent means you know the fact without slowing down. 7 x 8 should pop into your head as 56, the same way you know your own name.

Big idea: fluent does not mean fast guessing. It means quick and correct because the fact is yours.
02

Strategy 1: use a fact you already know

Hard facts get easy when you build them from friendly ones. You break the tough fact into pieces, solve each piece, and add.

Build 7 x 8 from 7 x 4
7 x 8 = (7 x 4) + (7 x 4) = 28 + 28 = 56
8 is just two groups of 4. So 7 x 8 is two groups of 7 x 4.
Try it: 6 x 9 feels tricky. Use 6 x 10 = 60, then take away one 6. 60 - 6 = 54.
03

Strategy 2: times and divide are linked

Every multiplication fact gives you a matching division fact. If you know one, you know the other.

7 x 8 = 56
56 / 7 = 8 and 56 / 8 = 7
6 x 9 = 54
54 / 6 = 9 and 54 / 9 = 6
4 x 7 = 28
28 / 4 = 7 and 28 / 7 = 4
Why this helps: a division problem turns into a familiar times fact. 42 divided by 6 asks "what times 6 equals 42?" Answer: 7.
04

The one-digit times one-digit set

By the end of Grade 3 you should know every product of two single-digit numbers. There are 100 of them, but the patterns make them easy.

Three patterns that cut the work
Pattern A. Order does not matter. 7 x 8 = 8 x 7. That is one fact, not two.
Pattern B. Times 1 and times 0 are free. 9 x 1 = 9. 9 x 0 = 0.
Pattern C. Times 10 just adds a zero. 7 x 10 = 70.
05

Try it: name the strategy

Tap the smartest move for each fact.

1. 8 x 9

Times 10 is friendly. Drop back by one group of 8 and you have 72.

2. 49 divided by 7

A division problem is a backwards times fact. 7 x 7 = 49, so 49 / 7 = 7.

3. 6 x 7

Times 5 is a friendly fact. One more group of 7 brings you to 42.
