Unit fractions

A unit fraction is one equal piece of a whole. The whole gets cut into the same size parts. You take just one of those parts. That is the unit fraction.

By the end you can name a unit fraction from a picture and tell what each number in the fraction means.

What this lesson teaches Understand a unit fraction as the quantity formed by one part when a whole is partitioned into equal parts.
01

What a unit fraction is

A unit fraction has a 1 on top. The bottom number tells how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The top number tells how many of those parts you have.

Big idea: the word "unit" means one. A unit fraction is one piece out of all the equal pieces.
02

The two numbers in a fraction

Every fraction has a top and a bottom. They have names.

1/3
Top: 1 means you have one part. Bottom: 3 means the whole is in three equal parts.
1/8
Top: 1 piece. Bottom: 8 equal parts make the whole.
Quick tip: the bigger the bottom number, the smaller each piece. 1/8 is smaller than 1/3 because the whole is sliced into more pieces.
03

Equal parts matter

For a fraction to be true, the parts have to be the same size. If a circle is cut into four parts but one is huge and three are tiny, those are not fourths.

Check yourself: before you write a fraction, look at the picture. Are all the parts the same size? If yes, count them. If no, the picture does not show a fraction yet.

05

Try it: name the unit fraction

Read each clue. Tap the right fraction.

1. A whole pie is cut into 8 equal slices. You take one slice. What is the unit fraction?

The bottom is 8 because the pie has 8 equal slices. The top is 1 because you took one slice.

2. A bar is split into 3 equal parts. One part is shaded. What is the unit fraction?

Three equal parts means the bottom is 3. One shaded part means the top is 1.

3. Which unit fraction shows the biggest piece?

The smaller the bottom number, the bigger each piece. 1/2 is bigger than 1/8 or 1/10.
