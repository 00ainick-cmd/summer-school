Placing fractions on a line

Yesterday you read a marked dot on a number line. Today you go the other way. You start with a fraction and find its place on the line yourself.

By the end you can take any fraction, count the right number of jumps from 0, and put it in the right spot.

What this lesson teaches Represent fractions on a number line.
01

Read the fraction first

When you start with a fraction like 2/5, look at the two numbers. The bottom tells you how to split the line. The top tells you how many jumps to take from 0.

Big idea: the bottom builds the line. The top picks the spot. Build first, then count.
02

Three steps to place a fraction

Use the same plan every time.

Step 1
Draw a line from 0 to 1.
Step 2
Use the bottom number. Split 0 to 1 into that many equal jumps.
Step 3
Use the top number. Count that many jumps from 0. Put a dot.
Quick tip: if the fraction is 3/8, split into 8 first. Then count 3 jumps from 0.
03

Worked example: place 2/5

Bottom is 5, so split from 0 to 1 into five equal jumps. Top is 2, so count two jumps from 0 and drop a dot.

Placing 2/5 on the line
Five equal jumps. The dot at the 2nd tick from 0 is 2/5.
04

Worked example: place 4/8

Bottom is 8, so split into eight equal jumps. Top is 4, so count four jumps. The dot lands halfway, because four jumps is half of eight.

Placing 4/8 on the line
Eight equal jumps. The dot at the 4th tick is 4/8. That spot is also halfway from 0 to 1.
05

Try it: pick the right spot

For each fraction, tap the right tick number to land on.

1. You want to place 3/6 on a line split into 6 equal jumps. Which tick from 0?

The top of 3/6 is 3. Count 3 jumps from 0 and drop the dot on the 3rd tick.

2. You want to place 1/4 on a line split into 4 equal jumps. Which tick from 0?

The top of 1/4 is 1. Count 1 jump from 0 and put the dot on the 1st tick.

3. You want to place 5/8 on a line split into 8 equal jumps. Which tick from 0?

The top of 5/8 is 5. Count 5 jumps from 0 and put the dot on the 5th tick.
