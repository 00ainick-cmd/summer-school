Chapter three

Character Traits

A character's traits are not always stated. Look for clues. Listen to what they say. Watch what they do. Read what they think. Those clues show you who they are.

By the end you will name a character trait and point to the thought, the words, or the actions that show it.

What this lesson teachesDescribe the personality traits of characters from the thoughts, words, and actions.
01

Traits are shown, not always told

A trait is the kind of person a character is. Brave. Shy. Kind. Stubborn. The story often does not say the trait out loud. It shows the trait through what the character thinks, says, and does. You read those clues and figure it out.

Some traits you might find:

bravekindshycuriousstubbornhonestcarefulcaringfunnypatient
02

Three places to find trait clues

Every page gives you three kinds of trait clues. Train your eyes to spot them.

Clue 1, Thoughts
"I cannot let my little brother see me cry."
Trait shown: caring or protective.
Clue 2, Words
"You forgot your lunch. Take half of mine."
Trait shown: kind or generous.
Clue 3, Actions
She waited an extra hour just to make sure her friend got home safe.
Trait shown: patient and caring.
03

Try it: spot the trait and the clue

Read each short passage. Pick the trait that fits. Then pick the line that proves it.

04

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
A feeling is the same as a trait.
No. A feeling is for a moment. Sad. Mad. Happy. A trait is who the character is over time. Caring. Honest. Stubborn. Mia might feel sad today, but her trait might be kind. Watch what the character does over and over.
