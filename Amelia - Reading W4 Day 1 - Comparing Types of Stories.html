Week four, Day one

Comparing Types of Stories

Fiction comes in many shapes. A folktale, a fairy tale, a fable, a realistic story. They are all made up. But each kind has its own rules. A folktale may have talking animals. A realistic story has kids who go to a real school. When you know the type, you know what to expect.

By the end you will compare two kinds of fiction. You will name what is the same and what is different.

What this lesson teachesCompare and contrast key elements in various types of fiction
01

Two kinds of made-up stories

A folktale is an old story passed down for many years. It often has magic, talking animals, or a lesson at the end. A realistic fiction story is made up too. But it could really happen in our world. Kids go to school, families argue, a soccer team wins or loses.

Folktale

"A clever fox tricked a hungry lion. The fox sang a song that put the lion to sleep."

Realistic fiction

"Mia hid her notebook in her backpack. She did not want her brother to read her poems."

02

What is the same in all fiction

All fiction shares the same key parts. Every made-up story has these three:

Characters

The people or animals in the story. Mia, the fox, Coach Park.

Plot

The events. What happens, in order, from start to end.

Setting

Where and when the story happens. A forest, a school, long ago, today.

Theme

The big idea or lesson the story leaves you with.

These four go in the middle of the Venn diagram. They show up in every type of fiction.

03

What is different

Each type of fiction has its own rules. These rules go on the outside of the Venn diagram.

Folktale only

Talking animals. Magic. A clear lesson at the end. Set long ago, often in a far place.

Realistic only

Set today, in places we know. No magic. People act like real people. The plot could really happen.

04

Try it: same or different

Read each story clue. Then tap whether it belongs in Both, Folktale only, or Realistic only.

05

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
If a story has animals, it must be a folktale.
No. A realistic story can have a dog or a pet cat. The difference is the animal acts like a real animal. In a folktale, the animal talks and thinks like a person.
