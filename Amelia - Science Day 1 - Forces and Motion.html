Balanced and Unbalanced Forces

A force is a push or a pull. When two forces are balanced they cancel and nothing moves. When they are unbalanced, the object moves toward the stronger side.

What this lesson teachesPlan and conduct an investigation to provide evidence of the effects of balanced and unbalanced forces on the motion of an object.

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
If the box does not move, there are no forces on it.
Not true. With balanced forces, both pushes are still there. They are equal and point opposite ways, so they cancel out and the box stays still.
