Day 1 . Week 2

Life cycles

Every living thing has a path. It starts small. It grows. It can make new life of its own. Then it ends. That whole loop is a life cycle.

By the end you can name the four stages every life has, and compare three different cycles side by side.

What this lesson teaches Develop a model to compare and contrast observations on the life cycle of different plants and animals.
01

What a life cycle is

A life cycle is the set of stages a living thing passes through, from start to end and back again. Every life cycle has the same four big jobs: be born, grow, make new life, and end. The stages along the way are different for different creatures. A puppy and a butterfly do not look the same as they grow. But both are following a cycle.

02

The four big jobs

Every life cycle does these four things in order. The names of the stages change. The jobs do not.

1
Born
a new life starts (hatch, sprout, get born)
2
Grow
the body gets bigger or changes shape
3
Reproduce
make new life of the same kind
4
Die
the life ends. The cycle starts again with the new life.
Reproduce means to make a new one of yourself. A plant grows new seeds. A bird lays an egg. That is the only way a life cycle can keep going.
03

Butterfly cycle: four shapes

A butterfly does something amazing. It changes shape four times. This is called metamorphosis.

1
Egg
tiny, stuck to a leaf
2
Caterpillar
eats leaves and grows fast
3
Pupa
a hard case, called a chrysalis
4
Butterfly
flies, mates, lays new eggs

The caterpillar and the butterfly are the same animal. They look nothing alike. The pupa stage is where the change happens.

04

Two simpler cycles

Most life cycles do not change shape four times. A puppy grows up to be a dog. It still looks like a small dog when it is little. A plant goes from seed to grown plant in a more direct way.

Bird or dog
  • Hatch from an egg, or get born
  • Look like a small version of the adult
  • Get bigger and stronger
  • Have babies of their own
Flowering plant
  • A seed sprouts in soil
  • A small shoot grows leaves
  • The plant grows tall and makes a flower
  • The flower makes new seeds. The cycle starts again.
05

Same and different

All three cycles end and begin again. All three pass through the four big jobs. The differences are in the shapes and the time.

Comparing. A butterfly goes from egg to adult in a few weeks. A tree might take 20 years from seed to seed-maker. A bird takes a few months. The clock changes, but the pattern is the same.
06

Try one

A frog hatches from an egg as a tadpole. A tadpole has a tail and no legs. It swims. Later, the tail shrinks and legs grow. The frog hops on land. The frog cycle is most like the cycle of a ...

A frog changes shape between hatching and adult, like a butterfly. The tadpole and the frog are the same animal in different shapes.
