Day 4 . Week 2

Inherited and learned traits

A trait is something true about a living thing. Some traits come from a parent. Some traits come from where you live. Both make you who you are.

By the end you can tell whether a trait was inherited from a parent or shaped by the environment.

What this lesson teaches Construct scientific arguments to support claims that some characteristics of organisms are inherited from parents and some are influenced by the environment.
01

What a trait is

A trait is anything true about a living thing. The color of your eyes is a trait. The way a dog sits when it is told to is a trait. The height of a sunflower is a trait. Scientists ask one big question about every trait: where did it come from?

02

Two sources

A trait can come from two places. It can be passed down from a parent. We call that inherited. Or it can be shaped by what happens around the living thing. We call that influenced by the environment.

Inherited

  • Comes from the parents
  • You were born with it
  • You did not earn it or learn it
  • Examples: eye color, fur color, ear shape

From the environment

  • Comes from where the living thing lives
  • Built up over time by what it does
  • Can change if the environment changes
  • Examples: tricks a dog learns, plant leaning toward light
A trait can be both. How tall you grow comes partly from your parents and partly from the food you eat. Most real traits mix the two. For this lesson, pick the bigger source.
03

Sort some examples

Look at each trait. Did it come down from a parent, or did the environment shape it?

A golden retriever has soft golden fur.Inherited
A dog sits when its owner says "sit."Environment
A sunflower has a yellow flower head.Inherited
A houseplant leans toward the sunny window.Environment
A puppy has floppy ears like its mother.Inherited
A tree that grew in strong wind leans to one side.Environment
04

How to argue your case

A scientific argument has two parts. A claim is what you say is true. Evidence is the facts that back you up.

Claim: The puppy's golden fur is inherited.

Evidence: The puppy's mother has golden fur. The puppy had this color from the day it was born. No training or place changed the fur.

Test it. Ask, "If the puppy had grown up in a different home, would the fur color be the same?" If yes, the trait is inherited. If no, the environment shaped it.
05

Try one

A bean plant by a sunny window grew tall and bent toward the window. Its sister plant in a dark closet grew weak and pale. Both plants had the same kind of seed. The big difference between them is from ...

Same seeds, different places, different results. The environment shaped the difference. If both had gotten the same light, they would have grown the same way.
