Session 05

One Number, Five Faces

The same rational number can wear a fraction, a reduced fraction, a decimal, a percent, or an equivalent fraction with a different denominator. Today you learn to move between them on demand and to spot the same number in disguise.

What this session teachesGenerate equivalent representations of rational numbers.
01 / LEARN

Two moves cover everything

A fraction never changes if you multiply or divide the top and the bottom by the same nonzero number. That single move generates every equivalent fraction. Then conversion between fraction, decimal, and percent is just three short rules.

Equivalent   multiply or divide both by the same nonzero n: 1/2 = 2/4 = 5/10 = 50/100
Simplify   divide top and bottom by their GCD: 12/18 = 2/3
Fraction to decimal   divide top by bottom: 3/4 = 0.75
Decimal to percent   multiply by 100 and add %: 0.75 = 75%
Percent to fraction   over 100, then reduce: 40% = 40/100 = 2/5
Why it matters. A track split listed as a fraction is the same idea as the percent on a scoreboard or the decimal on a stopwatch. Fluency between faces lets you read any of them as the same value.
02 / LEARN

Multiplying by 1 in disguise

Multiplying 1/2 by 3/3 is multiplying by 1. The value cannot change. But the form does: 1/2 becomes 3/6. Every equivalent fraction is built by multiplying or dividing by some clever form of 1.

Simplifying runs the move in reverse. 12/18 has 6 as the GCD of top and bottom; dividing both by 6/6 (another disguised 1) gives 2/3.
