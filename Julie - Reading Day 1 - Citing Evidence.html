Session 01

Proving It With the Text

A good claim about a text is only as strong as the evidence behind it. The skill is not just finding a quote that relates, it is finding the one that most directly proves the point and leaves the least doubt.

What this session teachesDraw conclusions, infer and analyze by citing the textual evidence that most strongly supports an analysis of what the text says explicitly as well as inferences drawn from the text.
01 / LEARN

Strong vs weak evidence

Many quotes can relate to a claim. Strong evidence is specific and directly supports the exact point. Weak evidence is vague, off topic, or could back almost any claim.

02 / DO

Pick the strongest evidence

Read the short passage. Then choose the quote that most strongly supports the claim. Watch for quotes that are true but weak.
