Session 02

Word Meaning in Informational Text

Expository writers introduce content-specific vocabulary the moment a concept demands precision. The skilled reader does not stop. The definition is almost always sitting in the same sentence, waiting to be extracted.

By the end of this session you will determine the meaning of figurative, connotative, and content-specific vocabulary in informational texts by reading context cues, parsing affixes, and recognizing inline definitions.

What this session teaches Determine the meaning of words and phrases as they are used in the text, including figurative, connotative, and content-specific meanings using context, affixes, or reference materials.
01 / LEARN

Three layers of meaning

LayerWhat it isWhere you meet it
Content-specificA technical term whose precise meaning matters within a discipline.Science articles, history texts, legal briefs, economics.
FigurativeLanguage used non-literally for rhetorical effect.Op-eds, essays, journalism describing abstract phenomena.
ConnotativeThe emotional or evaluative weight a word carries beyond its denotation.Editorials, political analysis, persuasive writing.

All three layers appear in informational reading. The denotative dictionary definition is rarely sufficient on its own; the meaning in this text is what the standard demands.

02 / LEARN

Inline definition: the appositive pattern

Expository writers know their readers may not recognize specialized vocabulary, so they routinely supply the definition in the same sentence. The most common pattern is the appositive: the term, followed by a comma, followed by a phrase that restates the meaning. Recognize the pattern and the definition is yours without leaving the page.

SentenceTermInline definition
The cell relies on its mitochondria, the organelles that convert nutrients into energy.mitochondriaorganelles that convert nutrients into energy
The court invoked stare decisis, the principle of following established precedent.stare decisisthe principle of following established precedent
Inflation, the steady rise in average prices over time, eroded household savings.inflationthe steady rise in average prices over time
03 / LEARN

When inline help is absent: context and affixes

When the writer does not supply an inline definition, two strategies remain. Context cues are the surrounding words and sentences that constrain the term’s meaning by example, contrast, or consequence. Affixes are the prefixes, roots, and suffixes that carry portable meaning across many words.

AffixMeaningSample words
bio-lifebiology, biosphere, biopsy
-cracyrule bydemocracy, autocracy, plutocracy
anti-againstantibody, antithesis, antitrust
trans-across, throughtransmit, transcribe, transparent

A reader who recognizes that plutocracy combines pluto- (wealth) with -cracy (rule by) can infer "rule by the wealthy" without a dictionary.
