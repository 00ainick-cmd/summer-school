Session 01

Force, Mass and Motion

Push harder and a cart speeds up faster. Load it heavier and the same push moves it less. One relationship ties it together: acceleration equals net force divided by mass.

What this session teachesPlan and conduct an investigation to provide evidence that the change in an object's motion depends on the sum of the forces on the object and the mass of the object.
