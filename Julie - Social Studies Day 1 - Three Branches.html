Session 01

Three Branches, One Balance

The Constitution splits power into three branches so no single one can run the whole show. Each branch also holds checks on the others. That design is what keeps the system in balance.

What this session teachesApply the principles of rule of law, representation, separation of powers, checks and balances, and federalism to explain the purposes and functions of the Constitution.
01 / LEARN

Who does what

Legislative makes the laws (Congress). Executive carries out the laws (the President). Judicial interprets the laws and weighs them against the Constitution (the courts). Splitting these jobs is called separation of powers.
