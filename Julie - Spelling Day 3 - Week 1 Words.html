02 / STRATEGY

How to nail tricky spellings

Tactics
  • Identify the tricky letters and say them aloud as you write. In accommodate: double c, double m.
  • Use a mnemonic. Necessary has one collar (c) and two socks (s).
  • Look for hidden small words. Embarrass hides "bar" and "ass" (two of each letter at the doubles).
  • Write the word, cover it, write it again from memory, check.
  • If a word looks wrong in your draft, it probably is. Trust the instinct, then verify.

06 / WATCH

Don't fall for this

Common error
"Spellcheck will save me."
Sometimes. But spellcheck does not catch a correctly-spelled wrong word (like their / there / they're), and many academic words have spellings that auto-correct will leave alone if you wrote a different real word. The only reliable check is your own eye, trained on the patterns.
07 / RECAP

The move

Remember:
  • Identify the tricky letter cluster in each word and rehearse it.
  • Use mnemonics for the doubled letters.
  • Spellcheck is a tool, not a backstop. Verify by eye.
