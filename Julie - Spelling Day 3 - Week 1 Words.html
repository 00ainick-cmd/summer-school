02 / STRATEGY

How to nail tricky spellings

Tactics
  • Isolate the problematic letter cluster and articulate each grapheme aloud as you write. In accommodate, both the doubled c and the doubled m demand deliberate attention.
  • Construct a mnemonic device. The standard example: necessary contains one collar (c) and two socks (ss), an associative anchor that reinforces the orthographic pattern.
  • Identify embedded sub-words. Embarrass conceals "bar" and "ass" within it, both featuring the doubled consonants.
  • Employ the look-cover-write-check protocol: examine the word, conceal it, reproduce it from memory, then verify against the original.
  • If a spelling registers as visually anomalous within your draft, your intuition is frequently correct. Honor that signal, then verify against an authoritative reference.

06 / WATCH

Don't fall for this

Common error
"Spellcheck will save me."
Occasionally, yes. However, spellcheck systems fail to detect homophonic substitutions (such as their / there / they're), and many academic terms can be misspelled into other legitimate words that auto-correct will silently accept. The only consistently reliable safeguard is a trained editorial eye, capable of recognizing the orthographic conventions of formal English independently of software assistance.
07 / RECAP

The move

Remember:
  • Identify the problematic letter cluster within each word and rehearse it deliberately, articulating each grapheme as you write.
  • Deploy mnemonic devices to reinforce doubled letters and other counterintuitive orthographic features.
  • Treat spellcheck as a preliminary instrument rather than a definitive safeguard. Ultimate verification rests on a trained editorial eye.
