Lesson 01

Numbers One to Ten
一から十まで

Learn to read the numbers 1 to 10 in Japanese. You will see the kanji (the character), the romaji (how to say it), and the number it means.

What this lesson teachesI can identify the general topic and some basic information by recognizing practiced or memorized words, phrases, and simple sentences.
01

The ten number cards

Read each card: character, sound, number. Tap a card to flip between the character and its meaning.

02

Predict, then check

Look at the character. What number is it? Guess, then reveal.

03

Match the character

Read the character, then tap the number it means.

04

Watch out for this

Good to know
一 二 三 look like 1, 2, 3 lines.
That trick stops at three. 四 (four) is not four lines. You learn each character as a whole picture, not by counting strokes.
