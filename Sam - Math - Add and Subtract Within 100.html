Beat 1 · Hook

Bundle when full, unbundle when short

Two problems below. Tap each to see the move that makes it work: bundling ten ones, or unbundling a ten.

27 + 15 = ?
Ones: 7 + 5 = 12, so write 2 and carry one ten. Tens: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4. Answer 42.
42 - 15 = ?
Not enough ones, so unbundle a ten: 12 - 5 = 7. Tens: 3 - 1 = 2. Answer 27.
Line up tens with tens and ones with ones. When the ones reach ten, bundle one ten. When the ones are too few to subtract, unbundle a ten.
Beat 2 · What you will be able to do

Your goal today

  • 1Add two-digit numbers within 100, regrouping when the ones pass ten.
  • 2Subtract within 100, unbundling a ten when there are not enough ones.
  • 3Keep tens lined up with tens and ones with ones.
Why it matters: adding and subtracting within 100 is everyday math: money, scores, and lengths, and it is the base for working within 1000.

LEARN 2

When the ones pass ten

28 + 14. Add the ones: 8 + 4 = 12. What do you do with the extra ten?

12 ones is 1 ten and 2 ones: write 2 and carry the ten. Tens: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4, so 42.

LEARN 3

When there are not enough ones

53 - 27. The ones are 3 - 7, which you cannot do yet. What next?

Unbundle a ten: 13 - 7 = 6. Tens: 4 - 2 = 2. Answer 26.

Beat 12 · The big takeaway
SYNTHESIS

The one rule to keep

Bundle when the ones fill a ten; unbundle when the ones run short.

Keep tens with tens and ones with ones. Ten ones bundle up into one ten when adding; one ten unbundles into ten ones when subtracting.

CREDENTIAL

The mastery rule

The 2.NBT.B.6 micro-credential is awarded when both checks are met: a strong Form A, and a strong delayed Form B on a later day. The written explanation and the out-loud task are for learning and for an adult to review, not for self-grading.
