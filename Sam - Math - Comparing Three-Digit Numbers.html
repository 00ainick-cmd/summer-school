Beat 1 · Hook

The biggest digit does not always win

Which is bigger, 281 or 318? Tap each card to see the trap and the fix.

281 vs 318
Tempting: 281 has 8 tens and 318 has only 1 ten, so pick 281? No, that is the trap.
300 beats 200
Compare hundreds first: 3 hundreds beats 2 hundreds, so 318 is greater.
Always compare from the left. Hundreds outrank tens, and tens outrank ones. A single big digit lower down cannot beat a bigger digit in a higher place.
Beat 2 · What you will be able to do

Your goal today

  • 1Compare two three-digit numbers and place >, =, or < between them.
  • 2Compare place by place, starting at the hundreds.
  • 3Resist the trap of picking the number with the biggest single digit.
Why it matters: comparing numbers is how you order scores, prices, and measurements, and it is the heart of rounding and estimation later on.
The objective (verbatim standard)Compare two three-digit numbers using the symbols >, = or <.

Beat 4 · Teach (hundreds first)
LEARN 3

Start at the hundreds

Compare 318 and 281. Which is greater?

Compare the hundreds first: 3 hundreds beats 2 hundreds, so 318 is greater. The 8 tens in 281 cannot outrank the hundreds.
Beat 4 · Teach (the deciding place)
LEARN 4

When a place ties, move over

Compare 627 and 672. The hundreds tie at 6, so which is greater?

When the hundreds tie, move to the tens: 2 tens is less than 7 tens, so 627 is less and 672 is greater.
