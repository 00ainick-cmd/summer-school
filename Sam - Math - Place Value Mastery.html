Beat 1 · Hook

The same digit, two different worths

Here is a puzzle before we teach anything. Both numbers below have a 5 in them. Tap each 5 and see what it is really worth.

53
The 5 here is in the tens place. It is worth 50.
35
The 5 here is in the ones place. It is worth 5.
Same digit, ten times apart. The place a digit sits in decides its worth. That is the whole idea of this lesson.
Beat 2 · What you will be able to do

Your goal today

  • 1Build any number up to 999 out of hundreds, tens, and ones.
  • 2Say what each digit is worth, and why it is worth that.
  • 3Write a number as a numeral, in expanded form, and in words.
Why it matters: place value is how money, scores, ages, and every big number work. Once you own this, reading and adding big numbers gets easy.
The objective (verbatim standard)Understand three-digit numbers are composed of hundreds, tens and ones.

Three sizes of block

Each place has its own color. Ten of a smaller block snap into one of the next size up.

Ones = 1 Ten = 10 ones Hundred = 10 tens
Big idea: ten ones make one ten, and ten tens make one hundred. Bundling up is what place value is built on.

LEARN 3

Place gives value

Predict the value of the middle 5 in 553, then reveal.

Beat 4 · Teach (the tricky case)
LEARN 4

When a place is empty: zero

Build 7 hundreds, 0 tens, 4 ones in your head. Which numeral is it?

It is 704. With no tens, the 0 holds the tens place. Drop the zero and the 7 slides down to mean 74.
Beat 4 · Teach (stretch)
LEARN 5

Tens in all

How many tens in all are inside 250? The whole number in tens, not just the tens digit.

250 is 25 tens, because 25 times 10 is 250.

Beat 12 · The big takeaway
SYNTHESIS

The one rule to keep

A digit's worth = the digit × its place.

The 4 in 426 is worth 400 because it sits in the hundreds place. Move it, and its worth moves with it.
