Beat 1 · Hook

The jump picks the digit

Two jumps from the same number. Tap each to see which digit moves. Only one digit changes, unless it rolls over.

340 + 10 = 350
Plus ten changes the tens digit.
340 + 100 = 440
Plus one hundred changes the hundreds digit.
Adding ten changes the tens digit; adding a hundred changes the hundreds digit. The other digits stay put, unless a digit passes nine and rolls over.
Beat 2 · What you will be able to do

Your goal today

  • 1Add or subtract 10 in your head and see the tens digit change.
  • 2Add or subtract 100 in your head and see the hundreds digit change.
  • 3Handle the rollover when a digit passes nine, like 295 plus 10.
Why it matters: jumping by tens and hundreds in your head is the engine behind mental math, estimation, and counting money.
The objective (verbatim standard)Add or subtract mentally 10 or 100 to or from a given number within 1000.

Beat 12 · The big takeaway
SYNTHESIS

The one rule to keep

The jump picks the digit. At nine, roll over.

Add or take ten and the tens digit moves; add or take a hundred and the hundreds digit moves. When a digit passes nine it returns to zero and the next place up goes up by one.
