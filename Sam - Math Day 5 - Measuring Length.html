Day 5

Measuring length

Length tells how long. A pencil is short. A driveway is long. To find length, you pick a tool that fits the job.

By the end, you can pick the right tool to measure any length, and you know the units that go with it.

What this lesson teaches Measure the length of an object by selecting and using appropriate tools.
01

What is length

Length is how far it is from one end to the other end. You can measure a side. You can measure how tall. You can measure how wide.

A short thing has a small length. A long thing has a big length.

The big idea. You measure length by lining up a tool from one end of the object to the other end.
02

The right tool for the job

Three tools cover most jobs. Each one fits a different size.

Ruler

For small things. Up to about a foot long.

Yardstick

For medium things. About 3 feet long.

Tape measure

For big things. Across a room or longer.

03

Units that go with length

Units name the size of one step. Small steps for small things. Big steps for big things.

UnitUse it for
InchPencils, hands, small toys
Foot (12 inches)Books, chair seats, short ropes
Yard (3 feet)Floor mats, doorways, a bike
CentimeterCrayons, paper clips, fingers
Meter (100 cm)Tables, desks, a couch
Match the unit to the size. Inches and centimeters are for small. Feet and meters are for big.
04

Three steps to measure

Use these steps every time.

Step 1. Pick the right tool.

Step 2. Line up the 0 with one end of the object.

Step 3. Read the number at the other end.

Always start at 0. Not at 1. That is the most common mix-up.

05

Try one

You want to measure how long a crayon is. Which tool fits best?

A crayon is small, so a ruler fits best. You can measure it in inches or centimeters.
