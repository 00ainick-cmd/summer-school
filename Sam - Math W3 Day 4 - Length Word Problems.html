Week 3 . Day 4

Length word problems

Words tell a length story. You read it. You spot the numbers. You add or take away.

By the end, you can read a story about length and find the answer with plus or minus.

What this lesson teaches Use addition and subtraction within 100 to solve problems involving lengths that are given in the same units.
01

Read the story

First, read the whole problem. Slow. Picture what is happening.

Look for two numbers. Look at the units. They should be the same. Inches with inches. Feet with feet.

The big idea. Same units, then plus or minus. Inches plus inches stays in inches.
02

Plus or minus? Words tell you

Some words mean to add. Some words mean to take away.

Words for addWords for take away
in allhow much longer
totalhow much is left
put togethercut off
longer (added on)difference

If two things are joined or added, you plus. If one thing is taken away, you minus.

03

Worked example: a board

A board is 24 inches long. You cut off 9 inches. How long is the board now?

Cut off means take away. So minus.

24 minus 9 equals 15

The board is 15 inches now.

End with the unit. Your answer is 15 inches, not just 15.
04

Worked example: a string

A red string is 18 in. A blue string is 25 in. You tape them end to end. How long is the new string?

Tape them end to end means add.

18 plus 25 equals 43

The new string is 43 inches.

05

Try one

A hose is 30 feet. A pipe is 12 feet. They are joined end to end. How long is the whole thing?

Joined end to end means add. 30 plus 12 equals 42. The whole thing is 42 feet.
