Chapter one

Long Vowels and Short Vowels

Every vowel can make two sounds. A short sound is quick, like the a in cat. A long sound says the letter's own name, like the a in cake. Read on and your ear will learn the difference.

By the end you will hear long and short vowels in small words and know the trick that flips one into the other.

What this lesson teachesDevelop phonics in the reading process by distinguishing long and short vowels when reading regularly spelled one-syllable words.

Two sounds for every vowel

Common mix-up
You say the magic e out loud.
No. The e at the end is silent. You never say it. Its only job is to make the vowel before it say its name. kit has a short i; add a silent e and kite has a long i.
