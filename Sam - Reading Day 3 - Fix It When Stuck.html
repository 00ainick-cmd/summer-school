Chapter three

Stop and Check

Sometimes your eyes keep going but your brain stops. The words turn fuzzy. You feel lost. That is a signal. Stop. Use a fix-it move. Then keep reading.

By the end you will notice when reading stops making sense. You will pick a fix-it move and use it.

What this lesson teachesMonitoring comprehension and making corrections and adjustments when understanding breaks down.
01

Notice the fuzzy feeling

Good readers know when reading stops working. Your brain sends a signal. Watch for it.

Signs your brain is lost
  • You read a page and forget all of it.
  • You hit a word you do not know.
  • The story does not fit what is in your head.
  • You feel sleepy or bored fast.

When you spot a signal, do not just read on. Stop.

02

Three fix-it moves

Pick one. Or try them in order. Each one helps in a different way.

fix 1
Reread the part
Go back to the last spot that made sense. Read slow. Sometimes you missed a word the first time.
fix 2
Look at the picture
Pictures give clues. They tell you what is happening, who is there, and what mood the page has.
fix 3
Sound it out, slow
If one word is the snag, sound out each part. Bask-et. Then say it whole. Sometimes that is all you need.
03

Which fix-it fits?

Each kind of snag has a best fix-it move. Match the snag to the move.

snag
I do not remember the page I just read.
Best fix: Reread. Go back. Read slow this time.
snag
I do not know what the kids are doing.
Best fix: Look at the picture. It will show you.
snag
One word is too hard.
Best fix: Sound it out. Break it into beats.
04

Pick the best fix-it

Read each snag. Tap the best fix-it move for it.

05

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
If a page is hard, just keep going.
No. That makes it worse. Your brain falls behind. Stop at the snag. Fix it. Then go on. Two minutes of fixing saves you ten minutes of being lost.
06

The move

Remember:
  • Notice when the page stops making sense.
  • Stop right away. Do not push on.
  • Pick a fix-it: reread, look at the picture, sound it out.
  • Once it makes sense, keep going.
