Chapter three

Sentences Into Paragraphs

Sentences are like Lego bricks. One brick alone does not build much. Snap a few bricks that go together and you get a paragraph: a little group of sentences about one main idea. When the idea changes, you start a new paragraph.

By the end you will know what makes a paragraph one paragraph, how to find the topic sentence, and how to tell when a new paragraph should begin.

What this lesson teachesUnderstanding that sentences are organized into paragraphs to convey meaning.
01

What a paragraph is

A paragraph is a group of sentences that all talk about the same one idea. The first sentence is usually the topic sentence: it tells you what the paragraph is about. The rest are detail sentences: they tell you more about that idea.

Topic sentence: My favorite sport is hockey.

I like the speed of skating. I like that you have to think fast and pass to your team. Scoring a goal is the best feeling in the world.

Every sentence in that paragraph is about hockey. That is what makes it one paragraph.

02

New idea, new paragraph

When the writer switches to a different idea, the new idea gets its own paragraph. You can see it on the page: a fresh line, sometimes indented.

Paragraph 1 · About hockey

My favorite sport is hockey. I like the speed of skating. Scoring a goal is the best feeling in the world.

Paragraph 2 · About my dog

My dog Lola loves the snow. She runs in circles and tries to bite the snowflakes. She always wants me to throw snowballs for her.

Two ideas (hockey, my dog), so two paragraphs. If you tried to mash them together, the reader would feel lost.

03

Sort six sentences into two paragraphs

Each sentence belongs in Paragraph 1 or Paragraph 2. Read the sentence, then tap 1 or 2. The colors should make two clear groups.

04

Find the topic sentence

The topic sentence is the one that says what the whole paragraph is about. Pick it for each tiny paragraph below.

05

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
A new paragraph starts every time you press Enter.
No. A new paragraph starts when the idea changes. You can have a long paragraph that fills a whole page if every sentence is still about the same idea. The line break is just how it shows up on the page; the real rule is the idea.
06

The move

Remember:
  • A paragraph is a group of sentences about one main idea.
  • The topic sentence tells the main idea. It is usually first.
  • When the idea changes, start a new paragraph.
  • Every other sentence in the paragraph adds details about the main idea.
