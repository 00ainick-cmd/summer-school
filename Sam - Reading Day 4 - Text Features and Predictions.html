Chapter four

Text Features and Predictions

Before you read a book, the book is already talking to you. Look at the title. Look at the pictures. Look at the bold words. Look at the headings. These are text features. They give you clues. Smart readers use these clues to predict. Then they read and check.

By the end you will spot text features. You will use them to make a guess. You will tell when your guess was right.

What this lesson teachesUsing text features to make and confirm predictions, and explain why a prediction was not confirmed.
01

Text features are the clues on the page

Text features are parts of a book that are not just sentences. They help you find your way. They help you guess what is next.

Title
The big words on the front. It tells you the topic.
Heading
A small title above a part. It tells you what that part is about.
Picture
A drawing or photo. It shows what the text is about.
Caption
The little words under a picture. It tells you about the picture.
Bold word
A word in dark print. It marks a word to notice.
Table of contents
A list of parts and the page each part is on.
02

Predict, then confirm

A prediction is a smart guess. You look at the clues. You make your best guess. Then you read and check. To confirm means to see if the clues were right.

Sometimes a clue tricks you. The picture might show a hockey stick. You guess the book is about hockey. But the book is really about a kid who broke a stick and learned to build things. That is fine! Go back. Point to the clue. Say "the picture made me think hockey, but the book was about building."

03

Read three book covers

Look at the title, heading, picture, and caption. Make your guess. Then tap to see what the book is really about.

04

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
A prediction is just a wild guess. Any guess is fine.
No. A real prediction uses the clues. Say "I think this book is about dogs because the title says Lucky the Puppy. The picture shows a dog." If you can not point to a clue, it is not a prediction. It is just a guess.
05

The move

Remember:
  • Text features are clues. Title, heading, picture, caption, bold words.
  • Use the clues to make a smart guess.
  • Read the book to check your guess.
  • If you were wrong, point to the clue. Tell why it tricked you.
