Chapter five

Two Words Stuck Together

A compound word is just two small words pushed into one. Sun + shine = sunshine. The meanings of the two words help you find the meaning of the new word. Once you see it, big words get easy.

By the end you will spot a compound word. You will split it into two small words. You will use the two meanings to find the meaning of the whole word.

What this lesson teachesUsing knowledge of the meaning of individual words to determine the meaning of compound words.
01

What a compound word is

A compound word is one word made of two smaller words. Both small words can stand alone. Both still have a meaning when you split them.

sun
+
shine
→
sunshine

Sun is a word. Shine is a word. Together they make sunshine. The meaning? Light from the sun shining on you.

02

Split, then add

When you see a long word, try to find two small words inside. If you can, split them. Add their meanings. That is what the new word means.

bed + room
bedroom
a room with a bed in it
back + pack
backpack
a pack you wear on your back
cup + cake
cupcake
a small cake the size of a cup
snow + ball
snowball
a ball made out of snow
03

Compound or not?

Not every long word is a compound word. The trick is to split it. If both parts are real words, it is a compound. If not, it is not.

Compound
"toothbrush"
Tooth is a word. Brush is a word. Both real. It is a compound.
Not a compound
"basket"
Bask is not a word. Et is not a word. Not a compound.
04

Find the meaning

Look at each compound word. Use the two small words inside. Tap the meaning that fits.

05

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
If a word is long, it must be a compound.
No. Long does not mean compound. Test it. Try to split it into two real words. If both halves are real, it is a compound. Picnic looks long, but pic is not a word. So picnic is not a compound.
06

The move

Remember:
  • A compound word is two small words made into one.
  • Split the word in your head. Are both parts real words?
  • Use the meanings of both parts. Add them.
  • That is what the compound word means.
