Chapter two

Opposites and Same Meanings

Words can be friends. Some say the same thing. Big and large are like that. Some say the other thing. Big and small are like that. Both kinds help you read. Both kinds help you write.

By the end you will spot words with the same meaning and words with the opposite meaning. You will pick the right one for a sentence.

What this lesson teachesUsing antonyms and synonyms.
01

Same meaning words

Some words are like teammates. They say almost the same thing. You can swap one for the other and the sentence still works. These are called synonyms.

SAME
big = large
happy = glad
fast = quick
small = tiny
SAME
mad = angry
cold = chilly
sad = upset
jump = hop

Try it: "The dog is big." or "The dog is large." Same idea. The sentence still works.

02

Opposite meaning words

Some words point the other way. They are flip-flops of each other. These are called antonyms.

OPPOSITE
big vs small
hot vs cold
up vs down
day vs night
OPPOSITE
fast vs slow
happy vs sad
open vs shut
light vs dark

Try it: "The cup is hot." vs "The cup is cold." Now the meaning is flipped. That is an opposite.

03

How to tell which is which

Ask yourself one question. Does this word mean almost the same thing? Or does it mean the other thing?

fast
SAME: quick, speedy
OPPOSITE: slow
happy
SAME: glad, joyful
OPPOSITE: sad
big
SAME: large, huge
OPPOSITE: small, tiny
04

Why you need both

Same words help you read. If you do not know quick, you can think "oh, that is like fast." Opposite words help you read too. If a book says "not cold," you know it means hot.

Same and opposite words help you write. If you keep saying nice, you can try kind or sweet to mix it up. If you want to flip what you said, you can use the opposite.

05

Same or opposite?

Look at the word. Tap if the other word means the SAME or the OPPOSITE.

06

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
"They sound alike, so they are the same."
No. The meaning is what counts, not the sound. Big and bag sound alike but mean very different things. Always ask: do they mean almost the same thing, or the other thing?
07

The move

Remember:
  • Same-meaning words can swap in a sentence. Big and large.
  • Opposite-meaning words point the other way. Big and small.
  • If you do not know a word, think of a same word you know.
  • If a sentence says "not ___," think of the opposite.
