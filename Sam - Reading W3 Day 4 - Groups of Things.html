Chapter four

Groups of Things

Lots of birds in the sky. Lots of dogs at the park. Lots of kids on the same team. We have a special word for a group like that. A flock. A pack. A team. One word for the whole group. Easy.

By the end you will know the special group words. You will pick the right group word for the right thing.

What this lesson teachesUse collective nouns.
01

One word for a whole group

A group noun names a bunch of the same thing as one. You do not have to say "lots of birds." You can say a flock. One short word. Easy to read.

A flock of birds flew over the school.

The flock is just one word, but it points to many birds. Group nouns work like that.

04

See it in a sentence

Group words go before the thing they name. Like "a ___ of ___."

long wayLots and lots of fish swam by.
short wayA school of fish swam by.
long wayLots of kids from her class went on the trip.
short wayHer class went on the trip.

The short way says the same thing. One group word does the work.

05

Pick the right group word

Read each sentence. Tap the group word that fits the thing.

06

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
"Any group word works for any group."
No. A flock is for birds. A pack is for dogs or wolves. A herd is for cows. They do not swap. Each group word goes with its own thing. Match the word to the kind.
07

The move

Remember:
  • A group word names a whole bunch in one word.
  • Birds = flock. Dogs = pack. Cows = herd. Fish = school. Bees = swarm.
  • Players = team. Kids in school = class. Music players = band.
  • Use the group word that matches the thing.
