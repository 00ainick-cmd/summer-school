Chapter nine

Describing Words

A big dog runs fast. Two describing words. They do different jobs. Big tells you about the dog (a noun). Fast tells you about the running (a verb). Words like big are adjectives. Words like fast are adverbs.

By the end you will know an adjective from an adverb. You will spot them in sentences and use them in your own.

What this lesson teachesUse adjectives and adverbs in sentences.
01

Adjective: a word for a noun

An adjective tells you about a noun. It tells you what kind, how many, or which one.

see it
A red hat. A tall tree. Three kids. A soft blanket.

Red, tall, three, soft: all adjectives. Each one paints the noun.

02

Adverb: a word for a verb

An adverb tells you about a verb. It tells you how the action happens, or when, or where.

see it
Run fast. Speak softly. Read slowly. Sing loudly.

Fast, softly, slowly, loudly: all adverbs. Many adverbs end in ly. Some, like fast, do not.

03

The big difference

Here is the test. Ask: what kind of thing? If the answer is a noun, the describing word is an adjective. Ask: how is the action done? If the answer is a verb, the describing word is an adverb.

Adjective (describes noun)
Adverb (describes verb)
a quick rabbit
runs quickly
a slow turtle
walks slowly
a loud bell
rings loudly
a soft pillow
sings softly

Notice: the verb form often adds ly. Quick to quickly. Slow to slowly.

04

See them in sentences

Green box is the adjective. Gold box is the adverb. Both jobs in one sentence.

adjective + adverbThe big dog runs fast.
adjective + adverbA red bird sings loudly.
adjective + adverbThe small car moves slowly.
adjective + adverbA happy kid jumps high.
05

Pick adjective or adverb

Read the sentence. Tap the word that fits the blank.

06

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
Mixing up quick and quickly.
Quick is for a noun: a quick dog. Quickly is for a verb: runs quickly. Same idea, two jobs. Ask: am I talking about the thing or the action?
07

The move

Remember:
  • Adjective = describes a noun (a person, place, or thing).
  • Adverb = describes a verb (an action).
  • Many adverbs end in ly. Quickly, softly, loudly.
  • Ask: am I talking about the thing or the action?
