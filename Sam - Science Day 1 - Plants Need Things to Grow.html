Science lab . Grade 2 Standard 2.LS2.A.1
Day 1 . Week 2

Plants need things to grow

A plant is alive. It needs things. Take one away and the plant gets sick.

By the end you can name what a plant needs and tell what goes wrong when it is missing.

What this lesson teaches Plan and conduct investigations on the growth of plants when growing conditions are altered (e.g., dark vs. light, water vs. no water).
01

Plants are alive

A plant is a living thing. It is not a rock. It grows. It needs food and a drink, just like you. A plant gets its food in its own way. It uses three things from outside.

02

The three things

A plant needs light. A plant needs water. A plant needs soil. Take one away and the plant gets sick.

Light
from the sun
Water
from rain or a cup
Soil
the dirt at the roots
Air helps too. A plant pulls air in from its leaves. But the big three are light, water, and soil. Keep those names in your head.
03

Same seed, different home

A scientist plants three seeds. Same kind. Same day. She gives each one a different home. Then she waits one week. Look at what she sees.

Light. Water. Soil.
Green leaves. Tall stem. The plant looks happy. It is healthy.
Light. Soil. No water.
The leaves go floppy. The stem droops. The dirt is dry. The plant wilts.
Water. Soil. No light.
The plant turns pale. It leans toward any small light. The leaves stay small.
Light. Water. No soil.
The roots have nothing to hold on to. The plant falls over and dies.
04

Why scientists test one thing

The scientist gave each plant the same seed. The only thing she changed was one thing. That is the rule. Change one thing at a time. Then you know what made the change. If she had used three kinds of seeds, she could not tell. She would not know if it was the seed or the light.

05

Try one

A bean plant gets soil and light. But you forget to water it for a week. What will the plant look like?

The plant misses water. Its leaves go floppy and the soil gets dry. Without water, the plant wilts.
