Pick the Right Material

Standard 2.PS1.A.2Analyze data obtained from testing different materials to determine which materials have the properties that are best suited for an intended purpose.
01

From sorting to picking

Last time you sorted materials by their properties. A property is something you can observe: hard, soft, bendy, stiff, see-through, waterproof, magnetic. Today you do the next thing real engineers do. You pick a material for a job. You would not make a window from cardboard. You would not make a raincoat from paper. The match between job and material is the whole game.

03

How engineers choose

An engineer does three things, in order. First, they say the job out loud: what does this thing have to do. Next, they test the materials and write down the data: which materials are bendy, which are waterproof, which are strong. Last, they make a choice: pick the material whose properties match the job best.

04

One choice, all the way through

Job: build a spoon for hot soup.
  1. Job needs: stiff (so it scoops), strong, and not too soft.
  2. Test the data: foam is soft, paper bends in soup, wood is stiff and strong, ice melts.
  3. Choice: wood or metal. Both are stiff and strong. Foam and paper fail the test.
The property tags tell you what fits the job. That is data driving the choice.

06

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
The strongest material is always the best choice.
A steel window would be strong, but you could not see through it. The best material is the one whose properties match the job, not the one that is strongest overall.
07

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
If a material works for one job, it works for every job.
Cardboard is great for a cereal box. It would be terrible for a raincoat. Each job has its own short list of properties that matter.
08

What you learned

  • A property is something you can observe and test.
  • Engineers say the job, test the materials, then pick.
  • The right material is the one whose properties match the job.
  • Strong is not always best. Match beats strong.
