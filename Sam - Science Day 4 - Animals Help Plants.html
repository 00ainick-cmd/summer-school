Science lab . Grade 2 Standard 2.LS2.A.2
Day 4 . Week 2

Animals help plants

Plants cannot walk. They cannot fly. But they still find new places to grow. Animals help.

By the end you can name two big jobs animals do for plants and how each one works.

What this lesson teaches Develop a simple model that mimics the function of an animal in dispersing seeds or pollinating plants.
01

Plants need a hand

A plant stays in one spot. Its roots hold it down. So how does a plant make new plants in new places? Animals do two big jobs for plants. One is pollinating. One is spreading seeds. Let us look at each one.

02

Two big jobs

Pollinating
A bee picks up bits of yellow dust from one flower. The dust is called pollen. The bee flies to a new flower. The pollen falls off. Now that flower can make a seed. No bee, no new seed.
Spreading seeds
A bird eats a berry. The bird flies far away. Later the seed comes out and lands in new dirt. A new plant grows in a new spot. The bird gave the seed a ride.
Why the plant cares. A seed that falls right under the parent plant has no room to grow. The shade is too thick. Plants need to send their seeds to new spots. Animals help them do that.
03

The bee and the flower, step by step

Here is a model. A model is a small story that shows how a real thing works.

1
A flower has bright color and sweet juice called nectar.
2
A bee sees the color. It lands and drinks the nectar.
3
Yellow pollen sticks to the bee's body and legs.
4
The bee flies to a new flower. Some pollen falls off there.
5
That flower uses the pollen to start a new seed.
04

The bird and the seed, step by step

1
A plant grows a sweet berry with a seed inside.
2
A bird eats the whole berry.
3
The bird flies to a new place. Far from the first plant.
4
The seed comes out of the bird and lands in new dirt.
5
A new plant grows. The bird got a snack. The plant got a new home.
Other ways. Some seeds stick to fur. A burr in a dog's fur gets a free ride. Some seeds float on the wind. But many seeds ride inside an animal.
05

Try one

A squirrel buries a nut to eat later. It forgets where it put one. The next spring, a small tree grows from that spot. What job did the squirrel do for the plant?

The nut is a seed. The squirrel moved it and forgot it. That is spreading seeds. A new tree grows in a new place.
