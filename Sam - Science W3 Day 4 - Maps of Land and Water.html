Maps of land and water

A map is a small picture of a big place. It shows where land is. It shows where water is. It helps you see the shape of a place.

By the end you can name the parts of a map of land and water.

What this lesson teaches Develop a model to represent the shapes and kinds of land and bodies of water in an area.
01

What a map is

A map is a small drawing of a big place. It shows you the shape of land. It shows you where water is. You can hold a map in your hand even if the place is huge.

Tip. A map looks down from the sky. So a lake on a map looks like a blob. A river looks like a line. The shapes match what is really there.
02

Kinds of land

Land has shapes. Some land is tall. Some land is flat. Pick the right name for each kind.

Mountain
tall and rocky
Plain
wide and flat
Coast
where land meets sea
03

Kinds of water

Water has shapes too. Some water sits still. Some water moves. Some water is huge and salty.

Lake
water that sits still
River
water that moves
Ocean
huge and salty
04

Pick the right tag

Each name on the map is land or water. Look at each one.

A tall mountain with snow on top
LAND
A wide flat plain where grass grows
LAND
A river that bends through the valley
WATER
A round blue lake with no waves
WATER
The coast where the sand meets the waves
LAND
The big blue ocean to the east
WATER
Why a map helps. A map is a model. A model is a small copy of a real thing. Models help you think and plan, like a toy car helps you think about a real car.
05

Try one

A map shows a blue wavy line. The line bends across the page. It does not sit still. What body of water is this most likely to be?

A bending line that moves across the page is a river. A lake sits still. An ocean is big and wide, not a line.
