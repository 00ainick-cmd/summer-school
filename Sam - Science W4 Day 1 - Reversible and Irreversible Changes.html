Week 4 . Day 1

Reversible and irreversible changes

Heat and cold can change things. Some changes can be put back. Some changes can never be put back. Today you learn the names for both kinds.

By the end you can tell when a change can be reversed and when it cannot.

What this lesson teaches Construct an argument with evidence that some changes caused by heating or cooling can be reversed and some cannot.
01

What is a reversible change?

A reversible change is one you can put back. The thing goes one way. Then it goes the other way. Nothing is lost. Heat and cold can do this with some stuff.

Ice and water

Heat an ice cube. It melts to water. Put the water in the freezer. It turns back to ice.

Goes both ways. Ice melts. Water freezes. The same water.

Water and steam

Boil water on the stove. Steam rises. Catch the steam on a cold plate. It turns back to water.

Goes both ways. Water boils. Steam cools. The same water.
Tip. If you can heat it and cool it and get the same stuff back, that change is reversible.
02

What is an irreversible change?

An irreversible change is one you cannot put back. The thing changes for good. Heat is often the cause. Once it is done, you cannot undo it.

Dough to cookie

Mix flour, butter, sugar, and egg into dough. Bake it. It turns into a hard cookie. You cannot turn the cookie back into soft dough.

One-way change. Heating bakes the dough. There is no un-baking.

Egg cooks

Crack a raw egg. Crack a raw egg into a hot pan. The runny egg turns hard and white. You cannot turn a cooked egg back into a raw egg.

One-way change. Heat changes the egg for good.
Tip. Wood that burns to ash, a candle that burns down, a cake that bakes. All one-way changes. Heat broke them in a way you cannot fix.
03

Sort each change

Read each line. Tap the right tag. Is the change reversible or not?

An ice cube melts in a warm cup
REVERSIBLE
A piece of toast burns to black
NOT REVERSIBLE
Water boils into steam, then steam cools to water
REVERSIBLE
A raw egg is cooked into scrambled eggs
NOT REVERSIBLE
Chocolate melts in a hot car
REVERSIBLE
A log of wood burns to ash
NOT REVERSIBLE
Butter softens on a warm counter
REVERSIBLE
A cake bakes in the oven
NOT REVERSIBLE
The big idea. A reversible change goes both ways. An irreversible change goes one way for good. Both can be caused by heat or cold.
04

Try one

A kid leaves a stick of butter on a warm plate. It softens and goes flat. She puts the plate in the fridge. The butter gets hard again. Is this change reversible or not?

Reversible. Heat softened the butter. Cold made it firm again. The same butter, just back to where it started.
