Different Kinds of Maps

Maps have jobs. Some show roads, some show borders, some show mountains, some show the weather. Picking the right map is the first step to finding what you want.

What this lesson teachesIdentify the properties and use of different types of maps for a variety of purposes.
01

Every map has a job

A map only fits on the page if it leaves things out. The mapmaker keeps what helps with the job and leaves the rest behind. Look at the lines and shapes on each kind of map. Their look matches their job.

Road map

Shows roads, streets, and highways so you can drive from one place to another.

Political map

Shows borders between states or countries and marks the capitals with a dot or star.

Physical map

Shows mountains, rivers, lakes, and deserts. The land itself, not the borders.

Weather map

Shows what the sky is doing: sun, clouds, rain, snow. Today and the next few days.

There are more, too. A treasure map shows where something special is hidden. A park map shows where the trails and bathrooms are. The job tells you which one to grab.

02

What map do I need?

Read the job, then tap the map that does it. The map will say if it fits.

03

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
A map of the same place can look very different.
A road map of Missouri shows roads. A physical map of Missouri shows the rivers and the Ozark hills. They are both Missouri, just doing different jobs.
