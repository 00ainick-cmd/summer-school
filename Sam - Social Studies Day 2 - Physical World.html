Day 2

The physical world

The world has many kinds of land. The world has many kinds of water. A mountain is land that is high and rocky. An ocean is water that is wide and deep. Today you will learn the names.

By the end, you can name mountains, rivers, oceans, deserts, forests, and plains.

What this lesson teaches Identify and describe physical characteristics of the world.
01

Land you can see

The world has many kinds of land. Each kind has a name. Each kind has a look.

Land 1

Mountain

A mountain is land that is very high. It has a pointy top. It is made of rock. Mountains are taller than houses. Mountains are taller than trees.

Land 2

Desert

A desert is land that gets little rain. It is hot in the day. The sand is dry. Not many plants grow there.

Land 3

Forest

A forest is land with many trees. The trees grow close. Birds live there. Many animals live there too.

Land 4

Plain

A plain is land that is flat. It has grass. It has soft ground. Cows and horses can eat there.

02

Water you can see

The world has many kinds of water. Some water is big. Some water is small.

Water 1

Ocean

An ocean is very big water. It is wide. It is deep. The water in the ocean is salty. Ships sail on the ocean. Fish and whales live in the ocean.

Water 2

River

A river is water that flows. It moves over land. It runs down hills. A river is not as big as an ocean. The water in a river is not salty.

03

Land or water?

Look at these two cards. Land is dry. Water is wet. That is the big rule.

LAND

Mountain. Desert. Forest. Plain.

WATER

Ocean. River.

04

Try one

A place is very dry. It is hot. It has lots of sand. What is this place?

A desert is dry. A desert is hot. A desert has sand. So this place is a desert.
