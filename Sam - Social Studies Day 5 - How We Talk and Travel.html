Day 5

How we talk and travel

People need to send words. People need to move. Today you will learn the ways we do both. Some ways are fast. Some ways take time. Each way is good for some jobs.

By the end, you can name three ways we talk and three ways we travel, and tell what is good and bad about each.

What this lesson teaches Describe different types of communication and transportation and identify their advantages and disadvantages.
01

Ways we talk

Communication means sending words to a person. People do this in three big ways.

Mail

You write a note. You put it in an envelope. You drop it in a box. A truck takes it. The truck takes days.

GoodIt can hold a card or a gift. People keep mail to read later.
SlowMail takes a long time. You wait days for it to come.

Phone

You pick it up. You press the buttons. You talk. The person on the other side talks back. You hear them right away.

FastYou hear the voice right away. You can talk back and forth.
Not keptThe words go away when you hang up. You cannot read them later.

Computer

You type words. You hit send. The note goes far in a flash. The person can read it on a screen.

Fast and savedThe note gets there in seconds. The words stay on the screen.
Needs powerThe computer needs power. Without it, the note will not send.
02

Ways we travel

Transportation means moving from one place to a new place. People do this in three big ways too.

Walk

You use your feet. You go step by step. You go where you want. You do not need a key.

No costWalking is free. It is good for your body too.
SlowYou can only go where your feet can go. Long trips take a long time.

Car

You get in. You buckle up. You drive on a road. You can go many miles.

FasterA car is much faster than walking. You can take a load with you.
Costs moneyCars need gas. Cars need fixing. They cost money to run.

Plane

You go to a big stop called the airport. You get on. The plane goes up. It flies in the sky. It lands far away.

Very fastA plane can take you to far lands in hours, not days.
Costs moreA plane ride costs a lot. You also wait at the airport.
03

Pick the right one

Each way is good for some jobs. Slow ways are best for short trips. Fast ways are best for long trips. A car is good for town. A plane is good for far places.

For talk, a phone is best for fast back and forth. Mail is best for a card you want to keep. A computer is good when you have a lot to say.

Try one

You want to send a "happy birthday" note that has a drawing in it. The person can keep it on the shelf. Which way is best?

Mail is best. A note in the mail can have a drawing. The person can hold it. They can keep it.
