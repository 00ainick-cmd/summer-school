Three branches of government

Our country is too big for one person to run. So the job is split into three groups. Each group has a job. The three groups work together. They also watch each other.

By the end, you can name the three branches and tell what each one does.

What this lesson teaches Identify and explain the concept of branches and functions of government.
01

Make laws

The first branch makes the laws. Laws are rules for the whole country. This branch is called the legislative branch. The people in it are called Congress.

Branch 1

Legislative

Congress writes new laws. They talk. They vote. If most say yes, the law passes. Laws keep us safe. Laws are fair to all.

02

Lead and enforce

The next branch leads our country. It also makes sure people follow the laws. This branch is called the executive branch. The leader is the president.

Branch 2

Executive

The president leads the country. The president signs the new laws. The president also helps when things go wrong. To enforce means to make sure people follow the rules.

03

Judge

The last branch is the judges. They use the courts. They decide if a law is fair. They decide if a person broke a law. This branch is called the judicial branch.

Branch 3

Judicial

Judges sit in courts. They listen to both sides. They look at the law. Then they decide. The top court is the Supreme Court.

04

Three jobs, one country

Each branch has its own job. No branch can do all three jobs. That keeps any one part from being too strong.

MAKE

Legislative writes the laws.

LEAD

Executive enforces the laws.

JUDGE

Judicial reads the laws.

05

Try one

Who makes the laws for our country?

Congress makes laws. The president signs them. Judges decide what they mean.
