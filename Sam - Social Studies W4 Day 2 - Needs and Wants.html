Week 4 . Day 2

Needs and wants

People want lots of things. People also need some things. A need is not the same as a want. Today you learn to spot the two.

By the end you can tell a need from a want and give an example of each.

What this lesson teaches Explain the difference between needs and wants.
01

What is a need?

A need is something you must have to stay alive and well. If you do not have a need, your body or your safety is at risk. People have only a few real needs.

Shelter
a place to live
Food
to eat each day
Water
to drink each day
Clothing
to stay safe and warm
Tip. Food, water, shelter, and clothing. These are the big needs. People also need love and care from family, but at first you can use the four big ones.
02

What is a want?

A want is something you would like to have. It is fun or nice. But you can still live without it. Most things we ask for at the store are wants, not needs.

Try this. If you skip a want, you are fine. If you skip a need, you are not fine. Ice cream is a want. Plain water is a need. You can live without ice cream. You cannot live without water.

Big idea. Money is not endless. Most people buy their needs first. Then, if they still have money, they can pick a want.
04

Try one

A kid walks into the store with five dollars. Her mom says she can pick one thing. Which item is a NEED for the kid right now?

Water is a need. People must drink water to live. The toy and the candy are fun, but they are wants.
