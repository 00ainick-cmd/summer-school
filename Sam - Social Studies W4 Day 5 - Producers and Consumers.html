Producers and consumers

Some people make things or grow things. Other people buy things and use them. The first group is called producers. The second group is called consumers. Every kid and grown-up is one or the other at different times.

By the end you can spot a producer and a consumer in a story or a picture.

What this lesson teaches Identify examples of producers and consumers.
01

What is a producer?

A producer makes a thing or grows a thing. A producer also can give a service, like cutting hair or fixing a car. People pay producers for their work.

Producer of goods

A maker. Grows food, builds toys, sews clothes, bakes bread.

Farmer: grows apples and sells them.
Baker: bakes bread and sells it.

Producer of a service

A helper. Does a job for you, like fixing or teaching.

Doctor: helps you when you are sick.
Teacher: shows kids how to read.
02

What is a consumer?

A consumer is the one who buys and uses what a producer made. When you spend money on food, toys, or a movie, you are a consumer. To consume something means to use it up.

Buys goods

Picks up apples at the store and eats them.

Mom: buys bread at the bakery.
Kid: buys candy with her allowance.

Buys services

Pays for help, then enjoys what the helper did.

Dad: pays the barber for a haircut.
Family: pays for tickets to a movie.
Tip. Each person can be both. You are a consumer when you buy lunch. You can be a producer when you draw a card and sell it at a school sale.
03

Spot each one

Read each line. Pick the right tag.

A farmer grows corn and sells it
PRODUCER
A kid buys an ice cream cone
CONSUMER
A baker pulls fresh bread from the oven
PRODUCER
A family pays for tickets to a show
CONSUMER
A barber cuts a kid's hair for ten dollars
PRODUCER
Mom buys a coat at the shop
CONSUMER
A teacher reads a story to the class
PRODUCER
A dad orders a pizza for dinner
CONSUMER
The big idea. Producers and consumers need each other. Without producers, there would be nothing to buy. Without consumers, no one would buy what the producers made.
04

Try one

A girl saves five dollars from her chores. She walks into a bakery and buys a fresh roll. Right then, what is she?

She is a consumer. She is using her money to buy a thing the baker made. The baker is the producer.
