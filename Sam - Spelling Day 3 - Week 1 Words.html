Week 1 Spelling Words

Some words do not follow the rules. They are tricky little words you have to know by sight. This week we learn ten of them. Each one shows up in books and writing all the time, so once they stick, your reading gets faster and your writing gets stronger.

By the end you will spell ten everyday words from memory and use them in your own sentences.

What this lesson teachesSpell words using irregular spelling patterns.
01

This week's words

Read each word out loud. Look at how it is spelled. The tricky part is the letters that do not say what you expect.

02

Strategy tips

How to learn a tricky word
  • Say the word slowly. Look at each letter as you say it.
  • Cover the word. Try to write it from memory.
  • Uncover and check. Fix any letter that is off.
  • Notice the tricky letters. In said, the ai sounds like short e.
  • Use the word in a sentence so it feels familiar, not strange.

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
"I sound it out, so it must be right."
For most words yes. For these ten, the letters do not match the sounds the regular way. The only fix is to study them with your eyes, not just your ears.
07

The move

Remember:
  • Some words are spelled in a way that does not match how they sound.
  • Look at the letters, say the word, then write it from memory.
  • Check the tricky letters in the middle, like ai in said or ie in friend.
