Capitalize Days, Months, and Holidays

Monday is not just a word. It is the name of a day, so it always starts with a big M. So does Tuesday, July, and Thanksgiving. Names of days, months, and holidays always start with a capital letter. Even when they pop up in the middle of a sentence, the first letter stays big.

By the end you will spell ten day, month, and holiday names with a capital first letter every time.

What this lesson teachesCapitalize weeks, days, months, holidays.

02

Strategy tips

When to use a big letter
  • Day names get a big first letter. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
  • Month names get a big first letter too. January, February, all the way to December.
  • Holiday names get big first letters on every important word. Thanksgiving. Christmas. New Year's Day.
  • The big letter stays even when the word is in the middle of a sentence. "I see you on Monday." Big M, right in the middle.
  • Words like today, tomorrow, summer, or winter are not names. They stay small.

Watch out for this

Common mix-up
"Only the first word of a sentence gets a big letter."
No. Names of days, months, and holidays always get a big letter, even in the middle of a sentence. "I have a game on friday" is wrong. It should be Friday with a big F. Names are special. They keep their big letter all the time.
