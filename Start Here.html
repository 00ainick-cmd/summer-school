Program guide

Everything you need to know about how this program works.

How it works

Each kid has their own dashboard. They open it, pick a subject, and the program shows them the next lesson on their mastery ladder. They cannot skip ahead. They finish a lesson by passing Form A (a quick multiple-choice check, 7 of 8), then return after a day or two to pass Form B (the same skill, different items). Both passes lock in mastery and unlock the next lesson.

The system never grades the kid against an opinion. Mastery is auto-graded only. The written explanation and the out-loud performance task are coaching, not gates.

Subjects covered

  • Math. Verbatim Missouri Learning Standards. Atomic per-standard lessons with hands-on tools (block builders, number lines, comparison frames).
  • Reading. Decoding, comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, writing conventions. Grade 2 covered through the full ELA ladder; Grades 3 and 8 covered through Week 1 so far.
  • Science. Sorting, forces, matter, electric and magnetic forces. Week 1 days 1 and 2 per grade.
  • Social Studies. Geography (maps and grids), American history (branches of government, Declaration). Week 1 days 1 and 2 per grade.
  • Japanese. Day 1 across grades (numbers, colors, days of the week).

Privacy and saving

Your kids' work is saved on this device only. We do not have an account system. We do not have your data. That means:

  • Pick one device per kid and stick with it.
  • Bookmark this URL on that device.
  • Use the parent dashboard's "Download backup" button weekly. Save the file somewhere safe (email, cloud drive, phone).
  • If you switch devices, download the backup on the old device and "Restore from backup" on the new one.

This is harder than a login system, but your kids' learning history never goes to our servers. No data breach. No targeted ads. Their work is yours.
