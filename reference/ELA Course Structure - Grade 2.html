ELA Course Structure · Grade 2

A mastery-based scope for Sam's daily English Language Arts. Every objective below is a verbatim Missouri Learning Standard. Units, sequence, recipe, and aesthetic are curriculum-design choices, labeled as such, exactly like the math architecture.

Source standards: Missouri Learning Standards for English Language Arts, Grade 2 (DESE, 2016 revision). Strands: Reading Foundations, Reading, Language.

Backbone v1 · Reading Foundations, Reading, Language. The Writing strand (2.W) appends next.

How this fits the program

ELA runs daily across the fourteen-week summer. Unlike math, reading is not a single straight line: foundations and the reading process run alongside language and vocabulary work, spiraling all summer. Each atom below is one verbatim standard, taught to mastery with the same Diagnose, Learn, Practice, Mastery check, Advance cycle the math lessons use. Each earns a micro-credential.

2.RF Reading Foundations2.R Reading2.L Language2.W Writing (next)
E1Decoding and Vowels 2.RFProceduralStorybook3 objectives

The foundation of reading: turning letters into spoken words. Runs first and keeps spiraling as a daily warm up. Verbatim parent: 2.RF.1 Understand how English is written and read; 2.RF.3.A Develop phonics in the reading process by.

  1. 1
    2.RF.3.A.a◆
    decoding multisyllabic words in context by applying common letter-sound correspondences including: single letters, consonant blends, consonant and vowel digraphs and vowel diphthongs
  2. 2
    2.RF.3.A.b◆
    distinguishing long and short vowels when reading regularly spelled one syllable words
  3. 3
    2.RF.1.A.a◆
    understanding that sentences are organized into paragraphs to convey meaning
E2The Reading Process 2.RConceptualStorybook5 objectives

Making meaning from text: predicting, questioning, finding evidence, retelling, and self-monitoring. Verbatim parent: 2.R.1 Develop and apply skills to the reading process; 2.R.1.A Develop and demonstrate reading skills in response to text by.

  1. 1
    2.R.1.A.a◆
    using text features to make and confirm predictions, explain why not confirmed
  2. 2
    2.R.1.A.b◆
    asking and responding to relevant questions
  3. 3
    2.R.1.A.c◆
    seeking clarification, and using information/ facts and details about texts and supporting answers with evidence from text
  4. 4
    2.R.1.A.d◆
    retelling a story's beginning, middle, and end and determining their central message, lesson or moral
  5. 5
    2.R.1.A.e◆
    monitoring comprehension and making corrections and adjustments when understanding breaks down
E3Word Meaning and Vocabulary 2.RConceptualField guide4 objectives

Figuring out what words mean from their parts and their context. Verbatim parent: 2.R.1.B Develop an understanding of vocabulary by.

  1. 1
    2.R.1.B.a◆
    using prefixes, root words, and suffixes to determine the meaning of words
  2. 2
    2.R.1.B.b◆
    using knowledge of the meaning of individual words to determine the meaning of compound words
  3. 3
    2.R.1.B.c◆
    using context to determine the meaning of a new word or multiple-meaning word in text
  4. 4
    2.R.1.B.d◆
    using antonyms and synonyms
E4Grammar in Speech and Writing 2.LProceduralDefault (magazine)8 objectives

The parts of a sentence and how they work. Verbatim parent: 2.L.1 Communicate using conventions of English language; 2.L.1.A In speech and written form, apply standard English grammar to.

  1. 1
    2.L.1.A.a◆
    use nouns and pronouns in writing
  2. 2
    2.L.1.A.b◆
    use collective nouns
  3. 3
    2.L.1.A.c◆
    use common irregular nouns
  4. 4
    2.L.1.A.d◆
    use reflexive pronouns
  5. 5
    2.L.1.A.e◆
    use regular verbs
  6. 6
    2.L.1.A.f◆
    use helping verbs with regular verbs
  7. 7
    2.L.1.A.g◆
    use adjectives and adverbs in sentences
  8. 8
    2.L.1.A.h◆
    produce simple declarative, imperative, exclamatory, and interrogative sentences
E5Writing Conventions 2.LProceduralLab notebook6 objectives

The mechanics of putting words on the page correctly. Verbatim parent: 2.L.1.B In written text.

  1. 1
    2.L.1.B.a◆
    write legibly (print, cursive)
  2. 2
    2.L.1.B.b◆
    use dialogue that contains quotation marks
  3. 3
    2.L.1.B.c◆
    use apostrophes correctly for contractions
  4. 4
    2.L.1.B.d◆
    capitalize weeks, days, months, holidays
  5. 5
    2.L.1.B.e◆
    capitalize abbreviated titles of people
  6. 6
    2.L.1.B.f◆
    spell words using irregular spelling patterns
