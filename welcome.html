Before you set up your family

Four things to know about how this works.

Your kids' progress is saved on this device, in this browser. That's a feature, not a bug. Here's what it means for you:

  • ✓
    Pick one device per kid and stick with it. The iPad, the family laptop, whichever. Be consistent. Progress on Mom's phone does NOT show up on Dad's laptop.
  • ✓
    Bookmark this page right now. This URL is how your family gets back in. There is no email reminder, because there is no account.
  • ✓
    Back up your progress weekly. From your parent dashboard, click "Download backup." Save the file to your phone, drive, or email. If your iPad dies or you clear browser data, that backup is your recovery.
  • ✓
    Switching devices? Just transfer the backup file. Download from the old device, upload on the new one. Takes 30 seconds. No support ticket required.
Why so device-specific? Because your kids' data stays on YOUR devices, not on our servers. Better privacy, no logins, no risk of a data breach affecting your kids. Most online learning programs can't say that.
